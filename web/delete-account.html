Delete your Afghan Deals Pro account

This page is the official account-deletion resource for Afghan Deals Pro, published by the app developer.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to Afghan Deals Pro.
  2. Open Profile → Account → Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account, review the warning, and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the email address registered to your account. If your account uses a phone number, include that number with its country code. Use the subject “Account deletion request”. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

Your account profile, personal information, listings, favorites, chat participation, notifications, and reports associated with your account are permanently deleted or anonymized.

Retention: Information that must be retained for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations may be kept only for the period required for those purposes. Backup copies expire through the normal backup-retention cycle.
